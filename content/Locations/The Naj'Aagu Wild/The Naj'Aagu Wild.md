The Naj’aagu Wild covers a long stretch of wetlands in the western coast, primarily inhabited by a number of loosely affiliated nomadic tribes. These traveling groups are the recognized wardens of the region, living off the land with minimal disturbance to the existing ecosystem and ensuring that nature remains untouched by urbanization.

In fact, the Naj’aagu Wild is famously the largest area of Opeia to emerge unscathed by battles fought, empires conquered, and borders redrawn over the past couple of centuries.

![Naj'Aagu Wild](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/regwild.png)

Malicious outsiders encroaching into this territory face many a hazard from wild beasts to the volatile properties of the environment, and of course the wrath of those who benefit from extensive knowledge on how to exploit and avoid these dangers.

That’s not to say that the people of the Wild are necessarily hostile: many tribes are welcoming towards newcomers, some more than others. Survival in the Wild is a skill not easily acquired, but natives are willing to impart wisdom and offer protection to those they deem worthy. As a start, you have to have proper respect for the force of nature.

The Wild has a warm climate with a wet and dry season. The average temperature is relatively uniform throughout the year, with a notable increase before the rains. Most of the area is covered in swamp forests, with mangroves along the shoreline serving as transition from the marine to freshwater and terrestrial habitats.

Settlements are few and far between, though they’re considered to be a shared resource and on occasion multiple tribes would jointly occupy these sites together.

Many natives feel that religion is a necessary component for spiritual fulfillment. Rather than viewing themselves as subjects, the people of the Wild believe that their souls are bonded with the forces and virtues the deities embody.
