Among Opeians, Inlinbus is universally known as nothing short of a deathtrap. It is a stark, jagged, inhospitable landscape with unforgiving heights and a disorienting union of snowy peaks doused with perpetual rivers of lava flowing through.

Time was when dwarven clans occupied the Demilune Highlands, a mountain range named after its crescent shape. In the year 532 the dormant volcano Irearth erupted, and the resulting earthquake, smoke, and lava decimated the immediate populace. The surviving groups have since relocated, leaving the area deserted.

![Irearth volcano](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/inlinbus.png)

Irearth rests at an elevation of nearly ten thousand feet, surrounded by hundreds of small volcanic cinder cones formed by flank eruptions. The main crater is approximately a mile wide and contains a churning lava lake.

Select expeditions have been sent into the territory in the past, though interest has since declined due to the high mortality rate of these ventures—supposedly linked to frequent sightings of a pair of dragons patrolling the perimeter.

Nowadays, the prevailing policy is to avoid the region entirely.
