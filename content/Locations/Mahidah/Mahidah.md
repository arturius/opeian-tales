Mahidah is a kingdom in the south-eastern quadrant of the continent. Its territory stretches across a portion of the mainland’s southern coast, as well as an island group in the Machocour Sea known as the Mahidahian Archipelago.

With the exception of the main island wherein lies the capital, the kingdom mainly consists of desert terrain with consistently high temperatures all year long, even during wintertime.

Although it is overall the hottest, driest, and sunniest region of Opeia, it is a land of extremes: at the time of high sun, scorching, desiccating heat prevails, while during colder periods of the year, night-time temperatures can drop to freezing due to the exceptional radiation loss under the clear skies.

Godfall, a massive impact crater on the mainland left behind The Shattering is an outstanding landmark of the territory, both subject of fearful superstition and devout pilgrimage.

Mahidah is an old nation with a relatively peaceful history. The remote location coupled with a radical climate has proven to be far too unappealing to wage war over.

Though nomads can be found in considerable numbers and evenly spread, most of the population resides on the main island where a handful of oases make way for vegetation. For three months each year, a very special weather phenomenon happens here which transforms the usually arid environment into a spectacular rainforest. Seemingly overnight, barren slopes are turned into emerald green hills, lakes and waterfalls sprout forth, and the temperature drops to a cool mid-range.

The people of the kingdom are known for their courtesy and hospitality, attributing these qualities to a strong sense of community forged by the necessity to survive. Revelry isn’t regarded as a simple pastime; Mahidahian celebrations are centered around the gratitude for life and all that makes it worth living.
