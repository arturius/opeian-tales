Lore:

#### Summary
- Temples: [[Ovphy]]'s temple is more of an archive, with tomes including pre-Shattering accounts of [[Dalanbar]]; there are also temples of [[Iddal]], [[Besros]] and [[Ravara]] (S2, S18)
- Slums where [[Mirage]] handed out berries to children (S2); poorer folk bad-mouth the Academy and call its agents Empire boot-lickers (S25)
- Rumours: strange encounters in closed-down bathhouses, a vampire in a brothel, a baker handing out free bread (S5)
- Bathhouse (run by [[Baron Gustav Reimund]]): enchanted steam, high-pitched noise from the pipes, a howler fiend (S6); the creature cornered there bore the Academy's mark (S10)
- A sanctioned bell tower paid for by the Residency; the bell isn't magical but the door is (conjuration) and leads to a pocket realm (S13)
- Pathstow Keep: run by the [[Godfrays]] as pseudo-regents (S6); [[Edna]] and [[Zephyr]] were taken there (S2); guarded by Christopher and Danielle (S18)
- Even Star Academy: tour of 4 rooms with items (S3); robes are long and royal blue with the Academy emblem (S11)
- Cock and the Bull: owned by [[Baron Gustav Reimund|Gustav]] (S9); site of [[Wesley]]'s ambush (S7) and the private party (S21, S22)
- Helter Skelter: where the group stayed and met [[Radomir Trueblood|Radomir]] (S1, S3)
- Black Cat: run by [[Avellina]]; the party invested 515 GP and redirect letters there (S25)
- Tam and Heart: hands out free bread with [[Llum]] pouches; missing soldiers [?] (S5); door carries abjuration and evocation (3rd level+), and the loaves are all illusion-magic but disease-free (S9, S10); a fiendish female figure with ashen, cracked skin tends the hearth with imps (S11); the magical oven hides a holy symbol of [[Yhara]] at its coldest point (S12)
- The Bountiful Bosoms: run by [[The General]], headquarters of the [[Pathwalkers]] (S10)
- Pugsly's Magical Emporium: run by [[Pugsly]], a ratfolk shifter (S24)
