Once the largest kingdom of the continent, Peltragowe is often regarded with a level of scrutiny over its history of relentless conquest. The rather aggressive warfare of Peltran rulers has led to the absorption of many old nations, cultures lost to the ages.

It wasn’t until the early 10th century that this fearsome reputation was tarnished by The Legion’s invasion of Tenedad, a long-standing suzerainty of Peltragowe. The first crack upon an invulnerable facade, some speculate that this event paved the way for the Peltran-Aurean War two centuries later.

The conflict ultimately resulted in the Peltran Kingdom shrinking, leaving many families displaced between the two powers. With a large portion of agricultural resources forfeit to the Aurean Empire, famine ravaged Peltragowe. Since then, Peltran rule has shifted towards more peace-aligned policies, hard-pressed to focus on adapting infrastructure. The interest in maintaining a substantial military force however hasn’t dwindled.

![Peltragowe](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/regpeltragowe.png)

Life in the kingdom depends heavily on one’s circumstances: a harsh class divide further underlines the contrast of the nobility’s comfort and the peasantry’s ongoing fight for survival. Many small towns are impoverished and bereft of sufficient provisions, while cities and outposts generally hold priority. For this reason, young commoners often wound up in the military or in the many dubious organizations of the seedy underbelly.

Religion is customary among the populace and most cities have a dedicated place of worship for the recognized eleven deities.

Peltragowe lies in the north-central region of Opeia, flanked by the [[Tressnal Mountians]] and the [[Laval Slopes]]. The capital is located in the southwestern quadrant. The kingdom has mixed terrain of hills, plains and an overwhelming percentage of forestland. There are four distinct seasons with warm and often humid summers and freezing cold winters. Precipitation is usually distributed throughout the year but dry seasons may occur.

The number of artificers has seen a steep increase in the past decades: due to the shortage of experienced mages, the remaining arcane schools have pivoted from traditional training and opened their doors to inquisitive minds looking to empower their craft with a touch of magic.
