The youngest nation of the continent, this crown jewel of arcane prowess seems to exist both as an enigmatic utopia and a tyrannical titan in the minds of Opeian folk.

Fittingly, the most distinct feature of the territory is the faintly rose-tinted dome encapsulating the land. Known simply as the Veil, this shimmery barrier prevents any means of travel to or from the area without the knowledge and permission of the governing body.

![The Aurean Empire](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/regaurean.png)

Aside from the precariously monitored trading endeavors and select few events ensuring passage, an individual born outside Aurean land has little chance of ever setting foot into the Empire—even then, it is rare that they’re welcome to stay indefinitely.

The lion’s share of Opeia’s arcane practitioners dwell here, upholding a sense of monopoly on weaving and pioneering magic. Due to the high value placed into academic knowledge, most of the population is capable of casting simple spells.

Permanent residents—known as the Engraved—are provided with food, housing, medicine, education and all other basic necessities free of charge, without contingency. Crime rates are exceedingly low as any sort of transgression can result in exile.

While the Empress has been the recognized leader of the nation since its conception, she is assisted by an executive branch. This council of seven are selected by vote every five years, though prospective candidates must meet stern prerequisites to qualify for taking on such a position.

With the exception of private businesses, most types of labor are commissioned and financed by the government: farmers, soldiers, craftsmen, academics, healers, even artists and performers often come about work through the council. Personal wealth is usually spent on novelty items and services.

The Empire rests in the north-eastern section of the continent, hugged by mountain ranges along its borders. With an overall rolling topography, most of the region consists of upland terrain interspersed with hills and valleys. Summers are long, hot and humid with light showers and peak seasonal rainfall in the hottest months. Winters are generally mild and dry.

Religious practices aren’t quite as focal in the lives of Aureans as elsewhere; the Empress herself is straightforward about her abstain from worship. Dedicated temples are limited to the capital city, while modest shrines can be found around smaller settlements.
