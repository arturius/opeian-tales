Opeia is the lone surviving continent of Dalanbar following the disastrous clash between the deities. The remaining habitable land stretches across the scarred and fractured world, a jewel on the ocean’s crown, its standing nations carved from vastly different landscapes and legacies.

![Map of Dalanbar](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/worldmap.png)

To the northeast, the elegant Aurean Empire thrives in a realm of misty waterfalls, glimmering pink blossom groves and marble structures, their sophistication and hedonism clashing quietly with the pragmatism of their western neighbor.

The forested, mountainous Peltran Kingdom sits at the continent’s heart as a political fulcrum. Its fortified cities and steely diplomacy make it both a beacon of stability and a pressure point for unrest.

Sprawling southwards along Opeia’s western coast is the territory known as the Naj'Aagu Wild. In these wetlands and tangled swamps tribal banners shift with the seasons, but reverence for nature unites the nomads who roam its paths.

In the arid south of Opeia lies Mahidah, a proud desert nation of hospitality. The crater known as Godfall on the mainland garners much attention and interest, but the kingdom’s heart beats strongest in the capital of Zalfari — an immense oasis of sandstone and vibrant colors nestled among lush gardens.

Across the continent in a northwestern direction, the crimson island of Tenedad bursts with flora and fauna just as enthralling and deadly as its fanged invaders.

Sitting below in volcanic haze, the Demilune Haunt and Inlinbus are unforgiving, blackened lands of grief and ash.
