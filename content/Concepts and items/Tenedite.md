Mystical resource from [[Tenedad]] that can be used to fuel magic rituals. Also has associations with blood magic and witches, but can be used to make powerful if not unstable weapons and armour. 

---
Lore:

#### Summary
- Powers the [[Pathmobiles]] (S2)
- Not strongly linked to abyssals; a catalyst, material and drug; experiments with it extend witchcraft and are passed on by oral history; it can even be incorporated into the body (S6)
- Used by [[Mirage]] in a blood ritual for [[Wulfric]] (S26)
- Mined in the Empire in quadrants, one north of [[Rahalo]] [?] (S31)
- Linked to the [[Aurean Vale]] and used in it (S34, S35)
- Hard to categorise: an organic, living crystal; can lie; has a cult (S35)
