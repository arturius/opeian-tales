#### GODDESS OF KNOWLEDGE

![Ovphy's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/ovphy.png)

Ovphy's godly force is linked to knowledge, intuition, and the architecture of magic itself. She is revered by scholars seeking mastery over both thought and spellcraft.

She is intolerant of reckless experimentation, believing reason must temper ambition lest the arcane unravel the world it seeks to improve. To earn her favor is not to master magic, but to understand its cost.

Known as the ultimate keeper of history, Ovphy is said to remember every truth ever uncovered. Even the most prideful arcanists often maintain a small altar in her name, seeking clarity rather than power.

Ovphy is represented by an open eye framed by two hands, signifying awareness and discipline. Her symbol is commonly etched into tomes and found in places of research.
