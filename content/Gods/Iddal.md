#### GOD OF WAR

![Iddal's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/iddal.png)

Iddal is the force of conflict and the unyielding will to persevere. He is not the god of mindless bloodshed, but of struggle earned through resolve and sacrifice. To Iddal, victory without hardship is hollow, and strength without trial is unproven.

His champions are remembered not merely as warriors, but as legends in their own right. For this reason, countless soldiers, mercenaries, and commanders turn to his worship, seeking not mercy, but the chance to ascend the heights of mortal might.

Though feared for his ferocity, veterans speak of Iddal’s blessing on the battlefield as a source of enduring reassurance. To fight in his name is to accept pain, fear, and loss and to press on regardless.

Iddal’s symbol is a composition of weapons with a helmet at the center. It is etched onto weapons, armor, and banners carried into battle. To bear it is to swear perseverance while staring down death itself.
