#### GOD OF UNITY

![Besros' symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/besros.png)

Besros embodies fellowship, camaraderie, and the strength found in shouldering burdens together. His domain encompasses cooperation, kinship, and the emotional glue that binds societies together.

Worship of Besros is most common among communities that value collective survival—families, militias, communes, and caravans. He is invoked during reunions, oaths of fellowship, and times of communal hardship.

Besros is represented by interlocking hands, symbolizing unity through mutual reliance. His mark is commonly woven into banners, or displayed in communal spaces. Among his followers, breaking bread beneath this symbol is considered a sacred act.
