#### GODDESS OF DARKNESS

![Sythar's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/sythar.png)

Sythar is the deity of ambition, secrecy, and the unseen currents of power that shape destiny. In her prime, she was revered for guiding mortals to seize control of their fate through cunning and resolve.

After The Shattering, her name became synonymous with treachery. Modern cults devoted to Sythar are outlawed, their doctrines often warping ambition into obsession, cruelty and dominance.

Sythar’s sigil is a solid circle (representing a sun) eclipsed by a crescent shape, representing the inescapable shadow within.
