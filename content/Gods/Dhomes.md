#### GOD OF ORDER

![Dhomes' symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/dhomes.png)

Dhomes oversees structure, law, and harmony. He upholds systems that allow civilizations to endure: rules, contracts, moral codes.

His followers believe that order is not oppression but the foundation upon which peace and progress are built. When the fabric of a realm fractures, it is Dhomes' divine duty to gather the fragments and bind them into coherence once more.

Dhomes is symbolized by a balanced arrangement of interlocking geometric forms, embodying stability and order. It is frequently displayed in courts, guild halls, and places of governance.
