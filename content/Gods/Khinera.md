#### GODDESS OF LOSS

![Khinera's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/khinera.png)

Khinera is the steward of grief, memory, and return. She is not death itself but the keeper of all that follows death, the threshold where sorrow is given meaning.

In her domain she tends to souls not as a judge, but as a caretaker. She weighs not deeds nor sins, but the burdens carried through a life. In her realm, loss is not punishment; her followers believe that sorrow is not something to be erased but something to be transformed, the seed of renewal. She does not promise reunion, only continuity.

Khinera’s symbol, a dove in flight with wings outstretched, represents freedom and peace of mind. Mourners often wear a dedicated symbol during periods of loss, later burning or burying it as part of a rite of release, acknowledging both love and letting go.
