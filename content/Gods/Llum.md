#### GODDESS OF LIGHT

![Llum's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/llum.png)

Llum is hope, joy, peace incarnate. She represents spiritual warmth; her portfolio is centered around selflessness and the inherent drive towards optimism as well as altruism.

She is revered as the guardian of the innocent and the patron of healers, caretakers, and those who endure life's trials with bravery and kindness. Her teachings emphasize mercy, perseverance, and the strength found in compassion.

Llum’s icon takes the form of a rising sun. It is a symbol of reassurance and safe passage. Lanterns bearing her mark are lit during births in anticipation of a new life.
