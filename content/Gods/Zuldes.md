#### GOD OF CHAOS

![Zuldes' symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/zuldes.png)

Zuldes is the deity of entropy, upheaval, and the unpredictable forces that reshape civilizations. He is not destruction for its own sake, but the force that ensures stagnation never hardens into permanence."

Though feared, his influence is believed to be necessary, for without disruption stagnation reigns and growth becomes impossible. In the cosmology of Dalanbar, Zuldes is the hand that breaks the old so that something new may be born.

Zuldes’ symbol is a broken circle with orbs in scattered orbit along the edges, representing disruption and transformative disarray. It is often painted in ash or chalk and rarely preserved as followers of Zuldes believe permanence contradicts his portfolio.
