#### GODDESS OF LOVE

![Ravara's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/ravara.png)

Ravara is the deity of passion and deep emotion. Her worship is intense, fleeting, and often personal.

While many revere her for romance and devotion, others curse her for heartbreak. Though to love under Ravara is to risk everything, her temples are often quite busy with ceremonies scheduled months ahead of time. Legend has it that getting wed in Ravara’s blessed favor is a key ingredient to a lasting union.

Ravara’s emblem is a rose in various stages of bloom, sometimes depicted with falling petals. It symbolizes love’s beauty and impermanence. Her followers wear the mark close to the heart, accepting that devotion may wound as much as it may empower them.
