#### DEITY OF NATURE

![Zeune's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/zeune.png)

As the counterpart to [[Ovphy|Ovphy]], Zeune governs not knowledge but life in motion, shaped by inevitability and experience rather than intention. They are revered by farmers, druids, and those who live in harmony with the land.

Unlike many deities, Zeune is rarely feared as their presence is felt in all living things. Followers believe that when balance falters, Zeune does not punish, but reminds - through winds, storms, fires, landslides - that all life is bound to the same cycle.

Zeune’s mark marries popular symbology of the elements of fire, water, earth & air, evoking the rhythm of seasons. It is often embroidered on ceremonial garbs, drawn on the earth or carved into farming tools.
