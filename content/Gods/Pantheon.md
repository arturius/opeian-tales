# The creation of the pantheon

## AS TOLD BY THE GODS DURING THE TWELVE'S DAWN

Long ago it was only [[Yhara]], a stream of consciousness in vast space. With her divine gift she conjured planets, moons, stars: a multitude of celestial bodies as well as the forces keeping them strewn together — and the mote of potential for other forms of life to develop.

Though eventually, in her solitude she too began yearning for company.

She channeled some of her essence and ripped it from her otherworldly spirit, molding it into life and imbuing it with a fraction of her power. This entity, born out of the sheer desire for connection, later became [[Besros]], the God of Unity.

![Ascendance](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/mythone.png)

[[Besros]] delighted in visiting young civilizations across the endless expanse of their domain, and on one particular planet he came across a pair of siblings: the great conqueror [[Iddal]], and his sister [[Ravara]], whom [[Besros]] had quickly taken a shine to.

He felt as though he’d discovered a truly significant soul, one that he couldn’t let slip through his fingers due to the constraints of mortality. He pleaded with [[Yhara]] to allow her into their realm.

[[Ravara]], however, was not willing to leave her brother behind.

The Queen Goddess, knowing that [[Besros]]’ geniality was quite simply the reason for his existence, begrudgingly yielded to his wishes and ascended the pair, gifting them powers to claim as their own. Soon enough, Besros and Ravara welcomed a daughter in their family: Llum.

[[Iddal]], in tribute to his niece, destroyed a barren planet, using its core to build a body, and transferred force from the blank veils to bring his creation to life. Thus, [[Sythar]] came into being, a divine entity intended to keep Llum’s company.

[[Yhara]], furious with [[Iddal]]’s indiscretion, punished him by banishing him from the gods’ home plane. She’d then keep him sealed away for eons.

[[Ravara]] was inconsolable, torn between the family she’d known her whole life and the family she’s found. Unable to persevere amidst this conflict, she fell into a deep slumber.

[[Yhara]], seeing [[Besros]]’ misery without his mate, took pity on Ravara once more and plucked those irreconcilable emotions from her heart, forging them into separate entities so they may not weigh on her any longer.

[[Ravara]]’s heartbreak birthed [[Khinera]], as did her gratitude Ikhter.

She awoke with those two now not a burden to be carried but confidants of hers to deconstruct her woes.

The imprisoned [[Iddal]] was restless and determined to break free, teetering on the edge of madness from being tucked away in the void for so long. Instead of waning, his strength and fortitude grew tenfold as he thrashed against his intangible shackles.

One particular instance of roaring out a bellowing war cry shook his cosmic oubliette so intensely that his seething tore a rift through it.

Elsewhere, on a far planet with a blossoming civilization, a gifted individual became a near-religious figure due to her immense and seemingly effortless control over both nature and the arcane.

Though she was unrivaled, her thirst for magical power couldn’t be satiated. Through her daring ventures and the pathway created by [[Iddal]]’s wrath, she had inexplicably stumbled upon the hidden realm — a feat [[Yhara]] never did anticipate from a plain mortal.

While the Queen Goddess’ back was turned, [[Iddal]] began conspiring with the woman to be liberated from his captivity.

With time and his divine boons at her disposal, she eventually succeeded in her quest.

Fueled and blinded by his fury, [[Iddal]] traveled back to the gods’ home plane seeking revenge for his damnation.

He promptly launched a reckless attack on [[Yhara]].

The battle was short-lived as the scales tipped heavily in the Queen Goddess’ favor. This time around, not even [[Besros]]’, [[Ravara]]’s, or even the others’ pleas found purchase as Yhara had branded Iddal far too volatile to exist.

Beaten, defeated, [[Iddal]] the relentless tactician had one more trick up his sleeve: in the crucial moment leading up to his coup de grâce, he invoked his champion’s name, allowing her to break through the divine gate and manifest on the plane.

Amused and lightly intrigued by such show of bravado, [[Yhara]], the patron saint of personal power, was willing to hear the mortal out in a trial to determine Iddal’s fate. Seeking to ascend, the woman had suggested that the Queen Goddess bind her soul to Iddal’s, reasoning that her cool rationale could douse the flames of his foolish rebellion once and for all.

[[Yhara]], fearing their combined strength, presented a counteroffer she’d thought far too demeaning to be entertained by any creature with a semblance of pride: ripping the woman apart, splitting her into two and stripping away her former self. Her power divided equally and their souls bonded together with Iddal’s, on the condition that neither the two halves nor Iddal may exist without one another. A defeat of one, the immediate downfall of all three.

Much to her surprise, the woman was quick to agree to the terms and [[Yhara]] had no choice but to keep to her word. The two halves then became [[Ovphy]] and [[Zeune]], ascended to live among the gods. Iddal was spared and permitted to remain on the home plane.

With [[Ravara]] in his ear, [[Iddal]]’s misgivings towards the Queen Goddess gradually began to evaporate as he shifted his attention to the pair entwined with his spirit. [[Ovphy]] and [[Zeune]] later birthed his twin sons: [[Dhomes]] and [[Zuldes]].
