#### DEITY OF FORTUNE

![Ikhter's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/ikhter.png)

Ikhter governs chance, opportunity, and the subtle bends of destiny that shape lives. They are neither benevolent nor cruel, but rather impartial to outcome and concerned only with the raw power of possibility.

Gamblers, travelers, and those who gamble everything on uncertain futures often invoke their favor, believing that tenacity invites fortune to turn.

Ikhter’s emblem resembles three shooting stars mid-descent. It reflects chance suspended in motion, the moment before fate materializes. Travelers and gamblers often carry charms etched with their sign as a superstition, regardless of the depth of their devotion to Ikhter.
