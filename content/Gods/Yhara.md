#### QUEEN GODDESS OF SINGULARITY

![Yhara's symbol](https://opeiantales.blog/wp-content/themes/magnumopus/assets/png/yhara.png)

Yhara is the deity of individuality, determination, and the boundless potential of the self. She represents the spark that compels creation and self-actualization, encouraging mortals to find meaning through personal aspiration.

Once revered as the guiding force behind innovation, exploration, and ambition, she was considered the divine patron of pioneers, visionaries, and those who dared to defy stagnation.

Following The Shattering, many believe Yhara perished. Her absence reshaped the spiritual landscape and much of her former following has since turned to Khinera.

Yhara’s sigil is a spiral crowned by a crescent, representing self-realization and the infinite inward journey. It is often etched into personal journals, tools, or worn as subtle jewelry by those who value autonomy and inner purpose. The spiral signifies growth without end, while the crescent marks potential yet unrealized.
